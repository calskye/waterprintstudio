philosophy

the idea you live by goes here, in a sentence or two.

I am entranced by water. As the foundation to life, it’s a force constantly straddling between gentle fluidity and ultimate strength.

I spent my most foundational years surrounded by water. Not only was my university nestled slightly inland from the Pacific ocean, but also had a grand total of 25 fountains scattered across campus which I spent my time there scouting out.

Each time I would wander across a new water feature, I’d hurry to slip out of my shoes and jump in. I wasn’t alone. Jumping into the gurgling depths was a center of carefree entertainment and connection. The sunny skies and towering palm encouraged an energy of freedom and fun.

For me, the fountains were also a refuge of meditation and peace. I’d lose myself daydreaming next to the crystal water, watching the ways the light bounced and refracted. Moments of clarity were gathered by my fingertips trailing the smooth surface that was never quite the same—an embodiment of chaotic and beautiful possibility.

I remember reading about the treasured textured rocks in traditional Chinese gardens that were forged through thousands of years resting on the bottom of lake beds where the waves would erode intricate patterns into the limestone surfaces. Gentle, slow, powerful, lasting, water leaves its imprint.

I hope you take away bits of joy, peace, and clarity from this part of my soul.
